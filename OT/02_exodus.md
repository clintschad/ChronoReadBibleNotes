# Exodus

## Chapter 1
### A new Pharaoh oppresses Israel, Jacob's descendents
Thursday, July 23rd, 2026
* Jacob's descendants, the Israelites, build for Pharaoh the store cities of [Pithom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pithom) and [Raamses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi-Ramesses).
* Pharaoh commands the Hebrew midwives, among them Shiprah and Puah, to kill the baby boys. They do not listen, and God blesses them by giving them families.

## Chapter 2
### Moses is born and later as a young adult escapes to Midian
Sunday, July 26th, 2026
* When [Moses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moses) is a baby, his [mother](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jochebed) puts him in a basket on the Nile and lets it float down to where Pharaoh's daughter is bathing who takes pity on the baby. Moses's [sister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miriam) asks Pharaoh's daughter if she should fetch a Hebrew nurse. Pharaoh's daughter says yes, and Moses's sister fetches their mother. The mother names the baby Moses because he was "drawn out of water."
* Moses kills an Egyptian beating a fellow Hebrew. Once he hears that others are finding out and Pharaoh either knows or will soon know, he flees to [Midian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midian#In_the_Bible).
* In Midian, Moses stands up for some women watering their father's flock when other shepherds try to run them off. The women tell their father [Reuel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jethro_(biblical_figure)) who invites Moses to stay with them. He gives to Moses his daughter [Zipporah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zipporah) as a wife who gives birth to a son. Moses names him "[Gershom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gershom)" because he (Moses) is a sojourner in a foreign land.
* Later, Pharaoh dies.
* The last verses say that God remembered his covenenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob and that He saw the people of Israel and that He "knew". What did God know?

## Chapter 3
### God talks to Moses through a burning bush
Saturday, August 1st, 2026
* Moses sees a burning bush on [Horeb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Horeb#Biblical_usage), the mountain of God.
* God says He will be with Moses when he asks Pharaoh to let the Israelites go.
* God says after Pharaoh lets the Israelites go, Moses will serve God on Mount Horeb.
* God tells Moses "I AM WHO I AM" is his name if the Israelites ask him what the name of the God of their fathers is.
* God also reiterates that he is Yahweh, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
* God also tells Moses to tell Pharaoh that He, the God of the Hebrews, has met with him and the elders of Israel.

## Chapter 4
### God gives Moses signs to perform and Moses returns to Egypt
Saturday, August 1st, 2026
* God gives Moses 3 signs:
  - Throwing his staff down to become a snake. He can pick up the snake and it resumes being a staff.
  - Putting his hand in and out of his cloak to make it leprous. Repeating makes it return to normal.
  - Taking water from the Nile, pouring it on the ground, and turning into blood.
* When Moses tells God he is not a great speaker, God tells him he will help him and Aaron and He will talk to Moses, Moses to Aaron, and Aaron to the Israelites.
* God tells Moses that the people looking to kill him are dead and to return to Egypt. Moses takes his wife and sons and places them on a donkey on his way there.
* Yahweh tells Moses to tell Pharaoh that Israel is his firstborn son [v22]
* With no leading context, Yahweh meets Moses at a lodging place on his way to Egypt and seeks to kill Moses. His wife circumcises their son, touches Moses' feet with it, and tells him "you are a bridegroom of blood to me!" Then Yahweh leaves Moses alone.
