# Numbers

## Ch 01 - Fighting Men Census
Friday, October 23rd, 2026
* Yahweh tell Moses to take a census of all men twenty years old and older who are of fighting condition from all the tribes (except the Levites). Yahweh picks out a person from each tribe to assist in counting the men. [v2-19]
* The number of fighting men per tribe varies from ~31k to ~74k [v20-43], with the total coming out to 603,550. [v44-46]
* The Levites are not counted in, but are in charge of tear down and setup of the Tabernacle. [v50-51]
* Any outsider coming near the Tabernacle during tear down or setup is to be put to death. [v51]
* The Levites are to camp around the Tabernacle to protect the people from wrath, presumably Yahweh's. [v53]

## Ch 03 - Duties of the Levites
Sunday, October 25th, 2026
* Yahweh takes all the Levites instead of all the firstborn. [v12]
* Yahweh takes all the Levites' cattle instead of the firstborn cattle of the people. [v41]
* Israel has the Levites and Yahweh has their firstborns. Yahweh wants to exchange His firstborns for all of Israel's Levites, but He has 273 more firstborns than Israel has Levites. So Yahweh sells the _excess_ firstborns to Israel at five shekels per person. This purchase is done by the people giving the money to Aaron and his sons. [v40-51]
* Duties of the Levites, by camp position relative to the Tabernacle court:
    - North: The [Merarites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merarites) guard and carry structural components of the tent and court (frames, bars, pillars, bases, pegs, cords, etc)
    - South: The [Kohathites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohathites) guard and carry items within the Tabernacle (ark, table, lampstand, altars, vessels, and screen).
    - East: Moses and the priests guard the Tabernacle sanctuary and protect Israel.
    - West: The [Gershonites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gershonites) guard and carry the coverings, hangings, and entrance screens of the tent and courtyard.

## Ch 04 - Pack and transport duties of the Levite clans
Monday, October 26th, 2026
* These duties were for the Kohathite, Gershonite, and Merarite men from 30-50 years old. [v3, 23]
* Items the clans are responsible for packing and transporting:
    - Kohathites: the Ark, Table of Presence, the Lampstand, the Altar [v4-15]
    - Gershonites: the curtains of the Tabernacle and the courtyard walls [v24-26]
    - Merarites: frame, lashings, pegs, etc of the Tabernacle and courtyard walls [v31-32]
* Kohathites were not to touch [v15] or look at [v20] the holy Tabernacle items they were packing and transporting.
* [Eleazar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eleazar), son of Aaron, was in charge of lamp oil, anointing oil, incense, and grain offering. [v16]

## Ch 05 - Test for Adultery
Tuesday, October 27th, 2026
* If a man suspects his wife of adultery, he is to bring her to the priest along with a grain offering. [v15] The priest creates a _water of bitterness_ by mixing dust from the Tabernacle floor with holy water. [v17-18] The priest makes the woman take an oath saying if she has committed adultery, then allow the bitter water to make her womb swell and her thigh fall away. [v21-22] The priest writes these _curses_ in a book, washes the curses into the bitter water, and makes her drink it, causing her pain. [v24] If the woman is guilty, her womb will swell, her thigh will fall away, and she will not be able to have children. If she is not guilty, she will be able to still have children. [v27-28]

## Ch 06 - Nazirite Vows and Priestly Benediction
Wednesday, October 28th, 2026
* [Nazirite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazirite)
    - a man or woman who is dedicating and consecrating themself to God for a time.
    - cannot consume vinegar, strong drink, or any grape products.
    - cannot cut their hair or trim his beard.
    - cannot go near a dead body.
    - must offer multiple sacrifices upon completion of their time.
    - upon completion of time, must shave hair at Tabernacle entrance and burn it with the peace offering.
* The [priestly benediction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priestly_Blessing) is at the end of the chapter.

## Ch 07 - Dedication Offerings for the Tabernacle's Altar
* The offerings were for anointing and dedicating the altar of the Tabernacle [v10] and to be given to the Levites and used for service of the Tabernacle. [v5-6]
* Each chief of the 12 tribes on different days offered silver and gold ware along with grain and oil. They also offered livestock. [v12-87]
* Yahweh speaks to Moses from above the Ark of the Covenant between the two cherubim [v89].

## Ch 08 - Cleansing of the Levites
* The Levites were _cleansed_ before going into Tabernacle service for Yahweh. They were to be sprinkled with special water, completely shaven, the people of Israel to offer them as a wave offering, and the Levites themselves to offer two bulls as sin and burnt offerings. [v5-22]
* The Levites served up until fifty years old and then retired, except for maintaining guard duty. [v23-26]
