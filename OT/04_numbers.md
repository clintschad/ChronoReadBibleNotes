# Numbers

## Ch 01 - Fighting Men Census
Friday, October 23rd, 2026
* Yahweh tell Moses to take a census of all men twenty years old and older who are of fighting condition from all the tribes (except the Levites). Yahweh picks out a person from each tribe to assist in counting the men. [v2-19]
* The number of fighting men per tribe varies from ~31k to ~74k [v20-43], with the total coming out to 603,550. [v44-46]
* The Levites are not counted in, but are in charge of tear down and setup of the Tabernacle. [v50-51]
* Any outsider coming near the Tabernacle during tear down or setup is to be put to death. [v51]
* The Levites are to camp around the Tabernacle to protect the people from wrath, presumably Yahweh's. [v53]

## Ch 03 - Duties of the Levites
Sunday, October 25th, 2026
* Yahweh takes all the Levites instead of all the firstborn. [v12]
* Yahweh takes all the Levites' cattle instead of the firstborn cattle of the people. [v41]
* Israel has the Levites and Yahweh has their firstborns. Yahweh wants to exchange His firstborns for all of Israel's Levites, but He has 273 more firstborns than Israel has Levites. So Yahweh sells the _excess_ firstborns to Israel at five shekels per person. This purchase is done by the people giving the money to Aaron and his sons. [v40-51]
* Duties of the Levites, by camp position relative to the Tabernacle court:
    - North: The [Merarites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merarites) guard and carry structural components of the tent and court (frames, bars, pillars, bases, pegs, cords, etc)
    - South: The [Kohathites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kohathites) guard and carry items within the Tabernacle (ark, table, lampstand, altars, vessels, and screen).
    - East: Moses and the priests guard the Tabernacle sanctuary and protect Israel.
    - West: The [Gershonites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gershonites) guard and carry the coverings, hangings, and entrance screens of the tent and courtyard.

## Ch 04 - Pack and transport duties of the Levite clans
Monday, October 26th, 2026
* These duties were for the Kohathite, Gershonite, and Merarite men from 30-50 years old. [v3, 23]
* Items the clans are responsible for packing and transporting:
    - Kohathites: the Ark, Table of Presence, the Lampstand, the Altar [v4-15]
    - Gershonites: the curtains of the Tabernacle and the courtyard walls [v24-26]
    - Merarites: frame, lashings, pegs, etc of the Tabernacle and courtyard walls [v31-32]
* Kohathites were not to touch [v15] or look at [v20] the holy Tabernacle items they were packing and transporting.
* [Eleazar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eleazar), son of Aaron, was in charge of lamp oil, anointing oil, incense, and grain offering. [v16]

## Ch 05 - Test for Adultery
Tuesday, October 27th, 2026
* If a man suspects his wife of adultery, he is to bring her to the priest along with a grain offering. [v15] The priest creates a _water of bitterness_ by mixing dust from the Tabernacle floor with holy water. [v17-18] The priest makes the woman take an oath saying if she has committed adultery, then allow the bitter water to make her womb swell and her thigh fall away. [v21-22] The priest writes these _curses_ in a book, washes the curses into the bitter water, and makes her drink it, causing her pain. [v24] If the woman is guilty, her womb will swell, her thigh will fall away, and she will not be able to have children. If she is not guilty, she will be able to still have children. [v27-28]

## Ch 06 - Nazirite Vows and Priestly Benediction
Wednesday, October 28th, 2026
* [Nazirite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazirite)
    - a man or woman who is dedicating and consecrating themself to God for a time.
    - cannot consume vinegar, strong drink, or any grape products.
    - cannot cut their hair or trim his beard.
    - cannot go near a dead body.
    - must offer multiple sacrifices upon completion of their time.
    - upon completion of time, must shave hair at Tabernacle entrance and burn it with the peace offering.
* The [priestly benediction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priestly_Blessing) is at the end of the chapter.

## Ch 07 - Dedication Offerings for the Tabernacle's Altar
Thursday, October 29th, 2026
* The offerings were for anointing and dedicating the altar of the Tabernacle [v10] and to be given to the Levites and used for service of the Tabernacle. [v5-6]
* Each chief of the 12 tribes on different days offered silver and gold ware along with grain and oil. They also offered livestock. [v12-87]
* Yahweh speaks to Moses from above the Ark of the Covenant between the two cherubim [v89].

## Ch 08 - Cleansing of the Levites
Friday, October 30th, 2026
* The Levites were _cleansed_ before going into Tabernacle service for Yahweh. They were to be sprinkled with special water, completely shaven, the people of Israel to offer them as a wave offering, and the Levites themselves to offer two bulls as sin and burnt offerings. [v5-22]
* The Levites served up until fifty years old and then retired, except for maintaining guard duty. [v23-26]

## Ch 09 - Passover Celebrated and Tabernacle Moving
Sunday, November 1st, 2026
* Some unclean men were barred from making their Passover sacrifice at the Tabernacle. [v7] Yahweh says they are not only allowed to but must keep these sacrifices and the passover. [v10-12]
* A cloud would be over the Tabernacle during the day and _had the appearance of fire_ during the night. [v15-16] As long as the cloud was stopped and resting over the Tabernacle, the people setup camp and stayed there. When the could lifted, the people would pack up camp and follow the cloud. The cloud could be stopped for a day or for longer periods of time, e.g. a month or longer. [v21-22]

## Ch 10 - Leaving Mount Sinai
Monday, November 2nd, 2026
* [Two silver trumpets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chazozra) were to be made. They were to be blown for summoning, breaking camp, warfare, feasts, and sacrifices. [v2-10]
* As the Israelites finally leave Mount Sinai, [Hobab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_minor_Hebrew_Bible_figures,_A%E2%80%93K#Hobab), son of [Jethro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jethro_(biblical_figure)) (Moses' father-in-law), wants to return to his homeland, but Moses convinces him to stay with them on their journey since he knows the wilderness they're in. Moses also tells Hobab he will be blessed when the Israelites are blessed. [v29-32]
* When the cloud that leads them would rest, Moses would say "Return, O LORD, to the ten thousand thousands of Israel." [v36]

## Ch 11 - The People Complain for Meat
Wednesday, November 4th, 2026
* Yahweh burns some outlying parts of the camp when he hears the people complaining. Due to this, they name the place [Taberah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taberah). [v1-3]
* With everyone complaining about no meat to Moses and Yahweh being angry, Moses talks boldly to Yahweh telling Him his burden is too much. [v10-15]
* Yahweh takes some of the _Spirit_ that is on Moses and places it on the seventy elders. The elders instantly prophesy for a bit and then stop. [v25]
* Two men of the seventy who had not gone to the Tabernacle (where they were supposed to all meet to receive the Spirit) nonetheless received the Spirit and started prophesying in the camp. [v26]
* Yahweh uses a wind to drop quail on both sides of the camp about a day's journey away. However, right before the people start to eat it, Yahweh sends a plague on the people and the people who craved the meat died and were buried there. They name the place [Kibroth-hattaavah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kibroth_Hattaavah). [v31-34]

## Ch 12 - Miriam and Aaron Speak Against Moses
Thursday, November 5th, 2026
* Moses siblings, [Miriam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miriam) and Aaron, speak out against Moses because of the [Cushite woman he had married](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zipporah#Numbers_12). They also appear jealous since they say, "Hasn't Yahweh also spoken to them before?" Upon hearing this, Yahweh summons all three of them to the Tabernacle and meets with them in His cloud form. [v1-5]
* Yahweh says that for other prophets, He speaks through visions and dreams, but to Moses he speaks directly and clearly, and that Moses _beholds_ His form. Yahweh also mentions that Moses is faithful to him. [v6-8]
* In His anger, Yahweh causes Miriam to have leprosy. Moses pleads with Yahweh to heal her. Yahweh responds that she at leasts needs to be cast out of camp for seven days and then she can be brought in again. I assume she is healed because He says she can be brought back in again. [v10-15]

## Ch 13 - Yahweh sends spies into Canaan
Friday, November 6th, 2026
* The [spies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twelve_Spies) are chiefs from each of the twelve tribes. [v2-16]
* They were to gather intel on the land: quality of the land (trees, fruit) and enemy information (strongholds, their strength, their numbers, etc). [v17-20] Their journey lasts forty days. [v25]
* Route of the spies (from www.preceptaustin.org)

![Route of the spies](https://www.preceptaustin.org/sites/default/files/images/spy.jpg)

* They go from their camp in [Kadesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kadesh_(biblical)) to [Lebo-hamath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labweh).
* They pass through [Hebron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebron), where descendants of [Anak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anak) who are from the [Nephilim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nephilim) live. The text gives names of some of these descendants that live in Hebron. It is also mentioned that Hebron was built seven years before [Zoan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoan) was built in Egypt. [v22]
* The spies bring back some grapes, figs, and pomegranates. The grapes they bring back are on a pole between two persons, so either there were many or they were very large. [v23-24]
* Upon their return, the spies say the land is good but also that the enemies in it are strong and well fortified. [v27-28]
* The spies seems afraid of the descendants of Anak, who are very large and are from the Nephilim. [v28, 33]
* [Caleb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caleb), however, is gung ho about taking the land. [v30]
