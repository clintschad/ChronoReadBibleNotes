# Leviticus

## Chapter 1 - Burnt offering laws
Wednesday, September 23rd, 2026
* The burnt offering can be either be a cow, sheep, goat, or birds. For the cow, sheep, and goats, it must be male and without blemish.
* The person making the offering is to lay his hand on the head of the animal, and this will atone for him. It is theorized that this is a transferrence of sin from the person to the animal. [v4]
* For the cow, sheep, and goats, the entrails and legs are washed before being burned.
* These burnt offerings are also _food offerings_ to Yahweh [v17].
